# Classic Homemade Pizza Recipe
Hmm not sure if this tastes great

A delicious homemade pizza that serves 4 people.

## Ingredients

### Pizza Dough
- 500g bread flour
- 7g instant yeast (1 packet)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 325ml warm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil

### Tomato Sauce
- 400g canned crushed tomatoes
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### Toppings (Classic Margherita)
- 200g mozzarella, sliced or shredded
- Fresh basil leaves
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Optional Extra Toppings
- Pepperoni
- Mushrooms, sliced
- Bell peppers, sliced
- Red onion, thinly sliced
- Black olives
- Parmesan, grated

Instructions

### 1. Make the Dough
1. Mix warm water, sugar and yeast in a bowl and let sit for 5 minutes until frothy
2. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt
3. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil to the flour
4. Mix until a dough forms, then knead for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover with a cloth
6. Let rise in a warm place for 1 hour until doubled in size

### 2. Make the Tomato Sauce
1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat
2. Saute garlic for 1 minute until fragrant
3. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil and sugar
4. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Season with salt and pepper and set aside

### 3. Assemble the Pizza
1. Preheat oven to 250C (480F) — as hot as it will go
2. Punch down the risen dough and divide into 2 balls
3. On a floured surface, stretch or roll each ball into a round base
4. Place on a lightly oiled baking tray or pizza stone
5. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the base, leaving a border for the crust
6. Add mozzarella and any extra toppings
7. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper

### 4. Bake
1. Bake for 10-12 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbling
2. Remove from oven and top with fresh basil leaves
3. Slice and serve immediately

## Tips
- The hotter the oven, the better the pizza — preheat for at least 30 minutes
- A pizza stone gives you a crispier base
- Less is more with toppings — don't overload or the base will go soggy
- Let the dough rest if it keeps springing back when you stretch it
- Leftover dough can be frozen for next time
