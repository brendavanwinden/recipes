/* # Classic Spaghetti Bolognese Recipe

A delicious homemade spaghetti bolognese that serves 4 people.

I think this is a great recipe!

## Ingredients

### Bolognese Sauce
- 500g ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 400g canned tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 150ml red wine
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tbsp olive oil

### Pasta
- 400g spaghetti
- 1 tbsp salt (for the water)

### To Serve
- 50g parmesan, grated
- Fresh basil leaves
- Black pepper to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Bolognese Sauce
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat
2. Saute onion, carrot and celery until soft (about 5 minutes)
3. Add garlic and cook for 1 more minute
4. Add ground beef and cook until browned
5. Pour in red wine and let it simmer for 2 minutes
6. Stir in canned tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano and basil
7. Simmer on low heat for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
8. Season with salt and pepper

### 2. Cook the Spaghetti
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil
2. Add 1 tbsp of salt to the water
3. Add spaghetti and cook according to package instructions (usually 8-10 minutes)
4. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining
5. Drain the spaghetti

### 3. Combine and Serve
1. Add the drained spaghetti to the sauce and toss to combine
2. Add a splash of pasta water if the sauce is too thick
3. Serve in bowls topped with grated parmesan and fresh basil
4. Finish with a crack of black pepper

## Tips
- The longer you simmer the sauce, the richer the flavor
- Always salt your pasta water — it should taste like the sea
- Save pasta water before draining, it helps bind the sauce to the pasta
- Use a good quality red wine you would actually drink
